# 0.9 mission: expectations registered before measurement

Protocol: `breaklint-090-metrics-v1`. Registered on 2026-10-04 before any mission product baseline or A/B run. Baseline implementation: npm `breaklint@0.8.0`; starting repository commit: `98e33c006cd6a0ef6a596caa5f18062b0ee74175`. No product content, labels, source paths or rendered product pixels belong in this public repository. The private work item holds those artifacts.

The target is useful layout findings: intentional chapter endings and other deliberate page breaks are accounted decisions; avoidable whitespace and broken figures, captions and references are explained with evidence and actionable advice. A clean verdict still requires actual measurement and sufficient coverage.

## Fixed release criteria

1. **Precision:** on the complete private adjudicated label set, the proportion of reported findings labelled `false-positive` or `intentional-acceptable` decreases by at least 50% in the candidate **all-rules arm compared with the baseline all-rules arm**. The candidate's default profile has at most 15% such findings. Every finding remains an individual record; display deduplication never changes the denominator. For each arm, F is the number of those two labels and N is every reported finding, including `real-but-cosmetic`; the adverse share is F/N. No unresolved `unclear` label is allowed at publication. N=0 or a baseline F/N=0 makes the relevant precision improvement unestablished, not a pass.
2. **Recall:** at least 50% of independently confirmed baseline misses in the whitespace or figure/caption/reference classes are reported by the candidate. Misses must have a second-family confirmation; unsupported classes remain explicitly listed. A zero eligible denominator establishes no recall claim and cannot supply the missing proof.
3. **No regression:** every baseline `real-defect` and `real-but-cosmetic` remains reported, unless a fresh Claude Opus adjudicator outside the implementation sessions records a concrete evidence-based reason for its removal. Codex cannot adjudicate a candidate removal. No baseline exit 0/1 becomes candidate exit 3/4 without independent proof that the new verdict is correct.
4. **Cost:** on identical current portable P08 print input, candidate elapsed runtime and peak RSS are no more than 120% of baseline. Use the median elapsed time of three isolated acquisitions per version and the maximum peak RSS of those three; report each underlying value. Acquisitions run serially, with Chrome version and orphan-process checks recorded. Repeat at least ten isolated times before and after for any suspected flake; do not expand budgets to obtain green.
5. **Gates:** all existing ordered repository and packed-consumer release gates pass on the exact candidate. The mutation gate remains at 100%. Every implementation package supplies observed red-before, green-after and mutation evidence plus a fresh independent verifier without open blocker/high findings.
6. **Independent usefulness:** complete, blinded, order-randomised report pairs for every judgeable product are reviewed by Claude Opus, Gemini and Grok. At least two families must find the candidate a real improvement with clearly higher value. Codex contributes to the candidate and cannot count as its independent judge.

The baseline all-rules arm uses the default coverage/gating semantics with every registry rule explicitly enabled, not the strict profile's changed coverage floors. The baseline default arm is separate. Locale follows the product. Candidate arms use the same input identity and renderer peers. Counts, coverage, duration and RSS come from actual command outputs; infrastructure failures do not become a precision result or a clean pass.

`real-but-cosmetic` is retained as a separate label and reader impact; it is not silently reclassified as a false positive. `unclear` labels block publication until resolved by the independent evidence adjudicator. Report both raw and per-rule three-family agreement. New candidate findings receive pixel labels from **Claude, Gemini and Grok**, never Codex. Their packs are anonymised to hide version and mixed with retained baseline packs in a seeded, order-randomised batch; the seed and complete assignment manifest are frozen before review. Never infer a label from a rule or its test.

Recall matching is performed by two fresh nonimplementing families (Claude and Gemini, or Grok if Gemini is unavailable but qualified). A finding matches a frozen miss only when both confirm the same defect class/mechanism and the same semantic source target or rendered object; page proximity alone is insufficient. Page shifts are allowed only with source/figure identity evidence. Each miss contributes at most one recovered unit, and a grouped finding must separately account for every object it claims to recover. Disagreement goes to a fresh Claude adjudicator with the full pack. This is a measured improvement on the disclosed development corpus; it does not establish unseen-document performance or calibration.

## Evidence and review protocol

- Inventory every requested paged and screen surface, including unpublished HTML. Record nonjudgeable and unknown inputs with reasons. Generate inputs only through documented product producers in a private scratch copy; preserve original byte hashes.
- Every baseline hit: canonical JSON, crop of the same acquisition's PDF with its box, neighbour-page thumbnails, minimal source excerpt, computed break CSS and paginator decision. A separate private snapshot tap must not change the published baseline bytes or measured CLI invocation; disclose any unavailable binding.
- Every **baseline** hit receives independent pixel judgements from Claude, Codex and a passing Gemini/Grok visual runner. Every error and disagreement also receives the other Gemini/Grok family. Candidate labels instead use the three nonimplementing families above. No sampling. Batches at most twelve hits; Grok at most six images and text below 50 KB. Judges identify a visible detail.
- Recall review covers every rendered page, disjoint Claude/Codex ranges with a preselected 20% swapped overlap recorded before reviewers see the pages. A fresh second-family reviewer confirms each candidate miss before counting it. Overlap disagreements and hit-label disagreements go to a fresh Claude evidence adjudicator, with reasons recorded. Freeze the labels before design and candidate comparison.
- Run the real Gemini/Grok random-code vision probe before Phase A; only a 4/4 positive with completed control can qualify a visual judge. Check actual model, inline image count and hashes for every visual call.
- Design is audited by fresh Claude Opus and Gemini before implementation. At most eight code/design audit findings per request. Settle conflicts with rendered or measured evidence, never majority voting.
- Auditors identify defects; they do not author candidate code or report copy. Implementation is Codex's responsibility. Every usefulness, release and surface reviewer runs in a new session and receives only the frozen acceptance task and evidence, not earlier audit verdicts. A reviewer that contributed implementation or authored the submitted artifact is disqualified.
- Render all changed human report surfaces across states, themes and viewports plus every A4 PDF page. Two fresh Claude Opus lenses and Gemini/Grok look at every rendered PNG. The Founder delegated the sight review for this mission on 2026-10-04; record AI reviewer identity and actual model, never a human look.

## Stop conditions

Stop a package after two verifier rounds keep finding new blocker/high defects. Stop publication on a missed metric, incomplete label/review coverage, missing gate or revoked sight-review delegation. A failed criterion remains a failure after measurement; no retrospective denominator or threshold change. An unavailable model is `UNKNOWN`, checkpointed without silent substitution. New runtime dependencies require written justification; schema changes require stamps, migration and tests. Money, secrets, delivered product replacements, announcements and autonomy switches are outside this mission.

Calibration, external trust infrastructure, Windows support and public redistribution of the private corpus remain parked. Every released rule stays truthful about `calibrated` and `remediation.tested`.

## Protocol clarification before the first product acquisition

A fresh Claude Opus protocol audit observed eight concerns before any product run began. The high concerns were resolved by specifying all-to-all pairing, nonimplementing candidate judges, complete denominator/unclear handling and evidence-based recall matching. Numeric thresholds did not change. The medium corpus-fit concern is an explicit limitation: design is intentionally derived from this corpus, as commissioned. Blinding, overlap adjudication and protected cosmetic defects are now explicit. The low family-overlap concern is dispositioned by fresh reviewer identity and the authorship boundary; design review is required by the commission and is not counted as implementation. Original audit and timestamp evidence remain in the private work item.
