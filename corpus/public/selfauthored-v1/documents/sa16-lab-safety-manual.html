Teaching laboratory 2.14: safety manual

Part 2: equipment, inspections and induction · for staff, demonstrators and students

1 Rules that apply at all times

  1. Lab coat, safety glasses and closed shoes are worn at all times behind the yellow line.
  2. No food or drink is brought into the laboratory, including water bottles.
  3. Nobody works alone. At least one trained demonstrator is present during every session.
  4. Every spill, however small, is reported to the demonstrator before it is cleaned up.
  5. Bags and coats are left in the lockers in the corridor, not under the benches.

Danger. Volatile or toxic reagents are only ever opened inside a fume hood whose airflow indicator shows green. If the indicator turns red during work, close the sash, stop work and tell the demonstrator.

2 Safety equipment and where to find it

The floor plan on the next page shows every item of safety equipment in the laboratory. Learn where the eye wash stations and the safety shower are before your first session; in an emergency there is no time to look for them. Both exits lead to the stairwell, and the assembly point is in the courtyard by the flagpole.
