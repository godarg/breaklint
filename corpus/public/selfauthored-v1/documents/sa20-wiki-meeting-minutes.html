Operations committee: minutes of the October meeting

Last edited by the committee secretary · 14 revisions · page status: approved

Present: chair, secretary, engineering lead, volunteer coordinator, two station leads. Apologies: treasurer. Meeting held in the carriage shed mess room, 19:00–21:10.

1. Minutes of the previous meeting

The minutes of the September meeting were read and approved without changes. The secretary will upload the signed copy to the archive section of the wiki.
Ok.

2. Matters arising

  • The replacement coupling for the brake van has arrived and is in the stores cage.
  • The signal box roof repair is still marked "blocked" on the works board because the scaffolding supplier has not confirmed a date.
  • Hi-vis vests in the new sizes have been ordered; the old ones will go to the recycling bank.

3. Ticketing system

The new ticketing tablets passed their trial at the booking office. The rollout - originally planned for May - moves to the start of the next season, so that volunteers can be trained over the winter rather than on a busy weekend.

The engineering lead summed up the trial: 'the tablets work, the chargers don't'. Two of the six chargers failed within a week and have been returned under warranty.

Training sessions will run on three Saturdays in February, each for up to eight people. The volunteer coordinator will send the sign-up link once the dates are fixed. The tablets need a firmware update before the sessions; the command, for reference, is tabctl update --channel "stable".

5. Any other business

The mess room television is to be replaced by a pair of the 27" screens that were donated by a local office. They will show the train running board and the weather radar. To switch the display over, press Ctrl - Tab on the small keyboard beside it.

The chair thanked the station leads for keeping the Halloween specials running despite the weather, and reminded everyone that the Christmas trains are fully booked.

The committee noted that the formula used for the volunteer hours report, x - y with x the rostered and y the cancelled hours, gives a misleading figure for the months with engineering closures. The treasurer will be asked to suggest a better measure.
