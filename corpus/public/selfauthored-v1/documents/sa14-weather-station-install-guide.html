Cinderwick WS-3 · installation guide
SETUP

Installing your weather station

This guide takes you from the box to the first reading on the console. Most people finish in an afternoon. You will need a drill with a 10 mm masonry bit if you mount the mast on a wall, a 13 mm spanner, and a second person to hold the mast while you tighten the clamps. Read the whole guide once before you start: the order of the steps matters, because the sensor array is much easier to wire and align on the ground than at the top of a mast.

Caution. Do not work on a roof or ladder in wind, rain or poor light. Keep the mast well away from overhead power lines: at least twice the height of the mast in every direction.

1 Choosing the site

The ideal site is open ground with nothing taller than the sensor within a distance of four times the height of the obstruction. In a garden that is rarely possible, so aim for the best compromise: the rain collector must not be sheltered by trees or eaves, the temperature probe should be over grass rather than paving, and the wind sensors should be as high and as clear as you can safely reach.

The console receives the sensor signal over a range of about one hundred metres in open air, much less through walls. Try the console in its intended place before you fix the mast, with the sensor array lying on the ground where the mast will stand.

2 Unpacking and checking the parts

Lay the parts out on a clean surface and check them against the list below. Fixings are packed in labelled bags; keep them in their bags until the step that needs them. If anything is missing, contact the dealer before you start rather than improvising with other screws: the stainless fixings supplied are chosen so that they do not corrode against the aluminium mast.

3 Assembling the sensor array

  1. Push the wind cups A2 onto the shaft until they stop, then tighten the set screw C14 with the 2.5 mm hex key.
  2. Slide the vane A3 onto the other shaft with the tail fin pointing away from the cross arm. Tighten the grub screw C13.
  3. Fit the radiation shield A4 below the array head and insert the probe A5 until it clicks.
  4. Connect the battery pack A7. The status lamp on the array flashes green three times.
  5. Place the console within five metres and wait for the first reading, which can take up to three minutes.

4 Mounting and aligning

Fix the wall bracket B4 or the pole clamps B5 first, then lift the assembled mast into place and tighten the clamps just enough to hold it. Use the spirit level on two sides of the mast before tightening fully. Turn the cross arm until the arrow on the array head points north, using the compass card rather than a phone, whose magnetometer is easily upset by the metal mast.

Route the sensor cable down the mast with a drip loop below the array, so that rain runs off the loop instead of along the cable into the connector. Fix the cable to the mast with the UV-stable ties every thirty centimetres.

5 First readings

Once the array is on the mast, the console should show the outdoor temperature and humidity within three minutes and the wind within one full turn of the cups. Rainfall reads zero until the first tip of the collector. If a value shows dashes for more than ten minutes, move the console closer to a window facing the mast and press the search key.
