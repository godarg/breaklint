Oakhollow Parish Planning Forum: verbatim record

Third open forum on the proposed Mere Brook footbridge · village hall · review copy for correction by speakers

This is the review copy of the verbatim record of the third open forum on the proposed footbridge over the Mere Brook. It is circulated to everyone who spoke so that they can correct the record of their own words before the final version is presented to the parish council. The record is printed double spaced, with line numbers, so that corrections can be written between the lines and referred to by number.

Please return corrections to the parish clerk within fourteen days. Corrections may clarify what was said but may not add new points; new points should be sent as a separate written submission, which will be considered alongside the record.

  • Speakers from the floor are identified by number in the order in which they first spoke.
  • Inaudible passages would be marked with a dash in brackets; there were none at this forum.
  • The chair and the project officer have already approved their parts of the record.

The drawings discussed at the forum are those dated in the council’s spring bulletin. The flood study referred to by the project officer is available from the clerk.
