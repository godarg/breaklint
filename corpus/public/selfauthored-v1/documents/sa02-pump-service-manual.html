Chapter 6 Diagnostics and fault clearing

K-40 metering pump, firmware 3.x · Quillfeather Hydraulics service documentation

6.1 Before you start

This chapter is written for service technicians who have completed the K-40 installation course. It assumes that the pump was commissioned according to chapter 4 and that the settings printout from commissioning is available on site. Most faults can be located with the status display and a multimeter; the few that need the service laptop are marked in the fault table.

WARNING The pump head may contain chemical under pressure even when the motor is stopped. Close the suction and discharge isolation valves and open the bleed valve into a suitable container before loosening any fitting on the wet side.

Work through a fault in the order the table suggests. The causes are listed from the most to the least likely, and the first action in each row is always the one that needs no spare parts. Record every action in the service log at the end of this chapter, including actions that did not help; the next technician will thank you for it.

6.2 Reading the status display

The two-line display shows the operating state on the upper line and the most recent fault code on the lower line. A fault code stays on the display until it is acknowledged, even if the condition that caused it has cleared. The status lamp beside the display repeats the state in colour so that it can be read from across the plant room.

LampMeaningPump state
Green, steadyRunning normallyDosing
Green, flashingWaiting for an external signalReady
Amber, steadyWarning active, dosing continuesDosing
Red, steadyTrip, dosing stoppedStopped
Red, flashingLeak alarm, dosing stoppedStopped, isolate

To acknowledge a fault, press OK once. To acknowledge and restart after a trip, hold STOP for three seconds and then press START. A trip that returns within one minute of a restart indicates that the cause has not been removed.

6.3 Fault code reference

The table lists every fault code that firmware 3.x can raise. Codes E01 to E12 are hardware or process conditions; codes from E13 upwards are raised by the controller’s own supervision. Rows are kept together on a page so that a code and its remedy are always read together.

E01Display shows E01 immediately after power-upSupply voltage below 190 V or a loose mains terminalMeasure the supply at terminal block X1. Tighten L and N. If the voltage is correct, replace the power board.
E02Pump runs but no stroke is felt at the headDrive coupling slipped on the motor shaftIsolate, remove the drive cover and retighten the coupling grub screw to 4 Nm.

6.5 Clearing an airlock

An airlock is the most common cause of a sudden loss of output. Air enters the head when the tank runs dry, when a suction fitting is loosened, or when the chemical degasses at high temperature. The pump keeps stroking, but the diaphragm only compresses the trapped air.

  1. Set the pump to manual mode at 100 percent stroke rate.
  2. Place a container under the bleed valve outlet and open the valve one full turn.
  3. Run the pump until a steady stream of liquid without bubbles leaves the bleed outlet.
  4. Close the bleed valve while the pump is still running.
  5. Watch the pressure gauge. It should rise to the working pressure within ten strokes.
  6. Return the pump to automatic mode and acknowledge fault E05.

WARNING The liquid leaving the bleed outlet is the dosing chemical. Wear the face shield and gloves listed on the safety data sheet, and dispose of the collected liquid as the plant procedure requires.
