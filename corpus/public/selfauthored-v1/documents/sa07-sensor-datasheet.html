VX-9 Environmental Sensor Module

Temperature, humidity and pressure in one 42 × 22 mm module · Datasheet, revision C

Overview

The VX-9 combines three calibrated sensors behind a single protective membrane and reports all three values over an I²C interface. It is designed for battery-powered field loggers, greenhouse controllers and building ventilation systems, where low sleep current and a long calibration interval matter more than the last decimal place.

  • Factory calibrated, certificate supplied with each batch
  • Hydrophobic membrane, rated IP67 when mounted
  • Sleep current below 1 µA
  • Programmable alarm thresholds on the INT pin
  • Two selectable I²C addresses
  • Auxiliary analogue input for a soil probe
  • Operating range −40 to 85 °C
  • Drop-in replacement for the VX-7 footprint
