# Rights and privacy of selfauthored-v1

- **Licence.** All files in this directory are first-party work released under the repository's
  MIT licence (`LICENSE` at the repository root), like the rest of the repository. There is no
  third-party material to which another licence could apply: no quoted text, no images, no
  fonts, no stylesheets and no scripts from anywhere else.
- **Rights status** in the vocabulary of `docs/validation/corpus-contract-v1.md`: provenance class
  `synthetic_first_party`, rights status `first_party`.
- **Fictional content.** Organisations, products, places, publications, projects and data are
  invented, and each document says so in its imprint line. Any resemblance of an invented name to a
  real one is unintended. No real company branding is imitated.
- **Privacy status** `synthetic_no_personal_data`. No person is named; people appear only by role.
  There are no e-mail addresses other than role addresses at `example.org`, no telephone numbers,
  no postal addresses, no identifiers of real people, no credentials and no analytics exports.
- **Paths.** The planted build-machine paths (`file:///srv/…`, `/var/…`, `/opt/…`, `/mnt/…`,
  `/docs/…`) were chosen so that none is a home directory and none names an account; the
  repository's secret scan rejects `/home/<name>` and `/Users/<name>` paths.
