# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short because they are meant to be read.

**Be civil about people and precise about work.** Criticism of code, measurements, thresholds and
documentation is the point of the project and is welcome without hedging. Criticism of a person is
not the same thing and is not welcome.

**Bring the evidence.** "This is wrong" is a start; the version, the input and the observed output
make it actionable. A claim without a way to check it costs everyone else the time it saved you.

**Assume the other person measured something you did not.** That is usually true here, in both
directions.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's
private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

**Scope.** This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in commit messages and in any other
space maintained by this project.

**Enforcement.** Report to **kontakt@dargel-solutions.de**. Reports are read by the maintainer,
Gottlieb Dargel, who is a single person and not a committee, and who will act on them: a warning, a
removed comment, or a block, according to what happened. Reports are kept confidential.
